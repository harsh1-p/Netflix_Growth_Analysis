# Netflix Growth Analysis | Excel Portfolio Project

## Overview

This project is an Excel-based practice analysis of Netflix's quarterly revenue performance across four geographic regions: UCAN, EMEA, LATAM, and APAC.

The goal is to analyze regional revenue trends, calculate growth rates, build an interactive-style dashboard using Excel charts and KPI cards, and develop data-informed business recommendations.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze quarterly revenue trends.
- Compare financial performance across geographic regions.
- Calculate and evaluate regional revenue growth.
- Create a dashboard with key performance indicators (KPIs) and charts.
- Translate analytical findings into business recommendations.
- Practice Excel-based data analysis and business intelligence skills.

## Key Findings

Based on the figures included in the practice workbook, from Q1 2024 to Q1 2026:

- **APAC:** Approximately 48% revenue growth.
- **EMEA:** Approximately 35% revenue growth.
- **LATAM:** Approximately 28% revenue growth.
- **UCAN:** Approximately 24% revenue growth.

Recent quarterly trends from Q4 2025 to Q1 2026 also show differences in regional performance, with APAC and LATAM recording growth while UCAN experienced a decline.

## Business Recommendations

- **APAC:** Explore opportunities to sustain growth through localization and market-specific engagement.
- **EMEA:** Maintain growth momentum and evaluate opportunities for further expansion.
- **LATAM:** Identify sustainable growth opportunities using market-level evidence.
- **UCAN:** Monitor recent revenue performance and evaluate retention, engagement, and content effectiveness.

These recommendations are analytical interpretations developed for this practice project and do not represent official Netflix business decisions.

This is a practice portfolio project created for learning and demonstration purposes. The figures and conclusions reflect the data recorded in the workbook and have not undergone a complete independent audit against every original source.

Source references are documented in the `Source_Log` worksheet. Growth percentages are approximate and may be rounded. The recommendations are intended for analytical practice and should not be treated as official company guidance or investment advice.
